Privacy & Cookies

Last updated: 4 September 2026

This page explains what personal information Pit Lane Mobile Tyres collects when you get in touch, why we hold it, and what your rights are. We keep it deliberately short and plain.

Who we are

Pit Lane Mobile Tyres is a mobile tyre-fitting business based in Blackwood, South Wales. For anything about your information, contact us on 07955 394341. We are the “data controller” for the information described here.

What we collect

We only collect what we need to give you a quote and carry out the work:

  • Your name and phone number.
  • Your vehicle registration and tyre size.
  • The address or location where you want the work done.
  • Anything else you choose to tell us about the job (for example, “flat on the front nearside”).

You give us this yourself when you call us, send a WhatsApp message or text us. We do not buy data about you or collect it from anywhere else.

Why we hold it, and for how long

We use your details to reply to your enquiry, give you a price, arrange a time and complete the fitting — and to keep a basic record of the work in case you contact us about it later. The lawful bases we rely on are taking steps to enter into a contract with you and our legitimate interest in running the business and keeping proper records.

We keep enquiry messages and job records for up to 6 years (to cover tax records and any warranty or dispute), then delete them.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information or share it for marketing. We only pass on details where we have to in order to do the job or meet the law — for example a tyre supplier (your tyre size, not your name), or our accountant and HMRC for tax. WhatsApp messages are carried by Meta Platforms under their own terms.

Cookies and this website

This website does not set any tracking or advertising cookies, and there is no analytics or advertising pixel on it.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your device’s IP address is sent to Google to deliver the font files. If we add website analytics in future we will use a privacy-friendly tool and update this page. The “Call” and “WhatsApp” buttons simply open your phone’s own dialler or WhatsApp app.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Just call or message us. If you are not happy with how we have handled your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this page

If we change how we handle your information we will update this page and the date at the top.
