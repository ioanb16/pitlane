Frequently asked questions

What is mobile tyre fitting?

Instead of driving to a garage, we bring a fully equipped tyre-fitting service to you. We arrive at your home, workplace or the roadside, fit your tyres on the spot, and take the old ones away, so you don't lose half a day sitting in a waiting room.

How quickly can you get to me?

For emergencies and roadside call-outs we come as fast as we safely can — usually the same day, and often within a couple of hours, depending on where you are and how booked up we are. Call us and we'll give you a realistic arrival time before you commit to anything.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Blackwood and cover roughly a 15-mile radius, including Newport, Cardiff, Cwmbran, Caerphilly, Pontypool, Risca and the surrounding towns and valleys. If you're not sure whether you're in our area, just call and ask.

How much does it cost?

You get a fixed, all-in price before any work starts, covering the tyre, fitting, balancing, a new valve and disposal of the old tyre — with no separate call-out charge. The fastest way to get a price is to call or WhatsApp us your car registration and tyre size (it's printed on the tyre wall, e.g. 205/55 R16) and we'll come straight back with an exact quote.

Is there a call-out charge?

No hidden call-out fee. The price we quote covers everything: the tyre, fitting, balancing, a new valve and taking your old tyre away. The price we agree before we set off is the price you pay.

What tyre brands can you supply?

Everything from budget and mid-range tyres to premium names such as Michelin, Continental, Bridgestone, Goodyear and Pirelli. Tell us your car and your budget and we'll give you a couple of sensible options to choose from.

Can you come out in an emergency?

Yes. We take emergency and roadside call-outs and aim to get to you as quickly as we can, with same-day service where available. Call us and we'll let you know the soonest we can be there.

Do you dispose of my old tyres?

Yes. We take your old tyres away and dispose of them responsibly as part of the service, so you don't have to worry about it.

How do I book?

Tap Call Now to speak to us directly, or message us on WhatsApp with your location, car registration and tyre size. We'll confirm the details, give you a quote and arrange a time that suits you.
